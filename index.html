Wall hold → longer hold → weight shifts → shoulder taps → freestanding → HSPU

Planche

Lean → frog stand → tuck → advanced tuck → harder progression

Pull-ups

2 → 3 → 4 → 5 clean reps, then muscle-up prep

Minimum day

When you're exhausted: 10 min handstand/L-sit practice + 10 min push-ups/legs + a walk. That's enough to keep the chain alive. A day like that is a success, not a compromise.

The 18.8kg weight covers rows, squats, split squats, and RDLs for the whole week. For the next 4 weeks, don't change this program, just execute it and record the numbers.
